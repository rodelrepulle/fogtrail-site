FogTrail

Walk to clear the fog. A friend sends you a place and you do not get a pin — you get a mood, a climb, and a glimpse through the trees. The map stays misted until you stand there and walk it.

Everything stays on your iPhone

FogTrail has no server of its own. Your steps, your walks and the fog you have lifted are stored in the app on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs in the app. Signing in with Apple and subscribing to FogTrail Pro are both optional and both go through Apple — none of your walking is sent there either.

Before you go

FogTrail points at real places, but the routes, notes and stills inside it are illustrative examples — not a survey, and not navigation. Read Walking safely before your first walk.
