You are responsible for what you put in them and for who you send them to: a FogTrail file carries the real name and the real line of the place, and the fog is enforced by the recipient's app rather than by cryptography. Seeded example content

The circle of walkers in the app (Mara, Jun, Tess, Nico) is a local demo created by the app on your device. The app says so on the Circle tab and in Settings. Sending a place to a real friend is a different action: it hands a file to iOS's share sheet, and you choose where it goes.
