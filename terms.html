Accounts and your Circle

Apple and Google sign-in create an optional account. “Continue with email” only suggests a name locally. Local walking works without an account; server friends, weekly boards and synced restore require one. Account data and optional uploads are described in the Privacy Policy. Delete the account from Me → Settings & privacy to remove its active server records.

Circle is friends-only. You choose whom to accept; there is no public feed, public ranking, live location or chat. You can remove or block a friend, decline an incoming place and delete a place you sent from the server. Blocking ends server access between those accounts; it cannot erase copies already saved by someone else.

Places you make and send

Your places, hints and photos remain yours. You give FogTrail permission to store and deliver a place only when you choose to send it through Circle. Circle delivery excludes photo attachments and personal notes in this version. File sharing can include them and uses the service you choose through the iOS share sheet.

A shared package carries the real name, destination and privacy-filtered line. Fog is enforced by the recipient's app, not by encryption. Send only to someone you know and trust, and only for safe, permitted ground. Do not include another person's private location, photograph or other content without their permission. Do not send unlawful, threatening, hateful, abusive or sexual content, or use FogTrail to follow, locate, harass or pressure someone. Contact support about unwanted or unsafe content.

A Privacy Zone and start/end trimming keep protected parts out of routes and Fogprint pictures you share or sync. These filters are a convenience, not a guarantee; check what you send. A manual backup keeps full routes, including protected areas, and is not encrypted by FogTrail. Keep it private.

Walking statistics and route sync are separate opt-in choices, off by default. Turning walking sync off deletes active synced walks/routes/stats; turning route sync off deletes its route points. Privacy-filtered restore cannot recreate withheld areas. Notifications are optional and delivery is not guaranteed; check Circle for your inbox.
