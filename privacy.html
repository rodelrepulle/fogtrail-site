Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Your walking is local by default. Walks, paths, steps, fog and memories stay on your device unless you choose an upload or share. Signing in alone does not switch on walking sync. FogTrail has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Circle is optional and friends-only. A signed-in walker can accept friends and send them a place. Walk statistics sync and route sync are separate choices, both off by default. Notifications are another optional choice; the inbox works without them. Fog is an app reveal effect, not encryption.

What stays on your device

FogTrail stores your completed walks, exact recorded routes, distance and elevation estimates, daily steps, discovered map cells, kept and received places, attached photos, memories, Privacy Zone and app settings in its private storage. Example places and the optional example Circle are sample content, not activity from real people.

Your personal map and history remain complete when sharing is privacy-filtered. Local photos and memories are not uploaded by Circle. Maps are provided by Apple MapKit, which communicates with Apple to supply map content under Apple's privacy policy.

Accounts and accepted friends

Signing in with Apple or Google creates an account containing your account identifier, walker name, avatar colour and preferences. Apple sign-in requests your name only, not your email address. Google supplies a name and an email address together; that address is stored for account authentication, not email marketing.

“Continue with email” only suggests a name locally: it creates no hosted account and does not save or transmit the address. “Continue without an account” remains available for local walking and discovery. Server features require a signed-in account.

Single-use friend links let you choose whom to invite. There is no public people directory, public feed or global ranking. We store friend requests, accepted friendships and blocks. An accepted friend can see your walker name, avatar colour and the weekly totals you choose to sync; they cannot browse your private walk history or restore routes. Removing or blocking a friend ends server access between those accounts.

Optional walking sync

Walk statistics sync starts only after you enable it. It uploads completed-walk dates, duration, steps, distance, elevation and new-fog totals, and daily activity summaries, linked to your account. Some steps may come from Apple Health. Accepted friends receive weekly aggregates through the Circle board, not your individual walks or a day-by-day activity log. Disabling walking sync deletes your synced walks, routes, activity summaries and any legacy synced revealed cells from the active sync tables and stops further uploads.

Routes need a second, separate opt-in. Route sync uploads precise location points for your own restore, after excluding your Privacy Zone and the start and end areas of walks. Route sync requires walking sync. Turning route sync off deletes the stored route points and any legacy synced revealed cells while leaving statistics sync on if you chose it. No friend can fetch these private restore records.

Sync is not an exact backup: withheld route portions cannot be recovered from the server. A privacy-filtered restored route cannot be turned into a new place to send. Your full private history remains on the original device.

Places you choose to share

You can send a place you made yourself through Circle. Our server stores its hint, recorded route, destination coordinates and distance/elevation estimates, your walker name and avatar colour, the sender/recipient account identifiers and delivery status. Only an accepted friend can receive it. The Circle package excludes the name you typed for the place, photo attachments, personal notes and any other free text beyond the hint. Privacy Zone and start/end filtering are applied before sending.

The recipient's app holds the actual place information to reveal it as they walk. Fog does not encrypt the package. A recipient who can read its underlying data can see the name and route. Check each place before sending, and share only with people you trust. You can delete a place you sent from the server. This cannot erase a copy already saved, exported or photographed by a recipient.

A .fogtrail file sent through the iOS share sheet travels through the service you choose, such as Messages or AirDrop, rather than through the FogTrail server. Those files may include a still or note you attached. The same route privacy filter applies; the service you select handles its own copy under its own terms.

Optional notifications

FogTrail asks iOS for notification permission only when you choose to enable place notifications. If you opt in, we store an Apple Push Notification service device token, linked to your account, to send place alerts through Apple. Alerts use generic copy and contain no place name, hint or location. Opening an alert opens Circle; it does not reveal a place. Delivery is best effort, and the inbox remains available without notifications.

Turning this option off removes the token for this device. You can also revoke notification permission in iOS Settings; FogTrail checks it when you return to the app. Explicit sign-out removes this device's registered token. After unexpected session loss, the app retries revocation without needing the expired account session. A random installation credential is kept only in this device's Keychain; the server stores its digest so another account cannot revoke or take over a token by knowing the token alone. Failed network revocations are retained locally and retried when the app can connect.

Location, Health and Motion permissions

Location is While Using the App, never “Always”. FogTrail records only while a walk runs, including with the screen locked; iOS shows its location indicator. Ending the walk ends background recording. Outside a walk, tapping Current Location takes one fix for distances or the Privacy Zone picker. Distance fixes expire after ten minutes and are never saved or uploaded; a chosen zone stays local.

Apple Health access is read-only, step count only. Motion supplies the phone's pedometer when Health is unavailable. Without optional walking sync, these readings stay local. With your sync choice, step summaries can be uploaded for your activity restore and accepted-friend weekly board. FogTrail never writes Health samples, uses Health data for advertising or marketing, sells it, or uses it for data mining.

Revoking Health, Motion or Location in iOS Settings changes future access; it does not delete previously recorded local or synced activity. Use the deletion controls below.

Storage, access and purposes

FogTrail-operated account and Circle data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in Singapore. Access rules restrict private activity to its owner, friend profiles and weekly aggregates to accepted friends, and sent places to their permitted parties. Data is used for account functionality, restore, optional weekly boards and requested place delivery. It is not used for tracking across apps or websites, advertising or marketing, and is not sold.

Data transmitted by these features is linked to your account: name, account ID, Google email when applicable, HealthKit-derived steps and fitness summaries, precise route/place location, shared content and optional device token. Optional collection is still declared in FogTrail's App Privacy information. Network providers also process requests needed to operate their services; their policies explain their handling.

Manual export and backup

Export my walks creates a JSON file locally and hands it to the share sheet. Export FogTrail backup creates an exact restore file with full routes, discovered areas, kept/received places, memories, Privacy Zone and local share history. A manual backup retains protected areas and is not encrypted by FogTrail: keep it private. Choosing a destination can upload it to that destination service. It is separate from optional privacy-filtered sync.

Shared Fogprint pictures exclude walk starts/ends and your Privacy Zone and display no map labels. Your own Fogprint remains complete.

Deletion and retention

Delete account in Me → Settings & privacy permanently removes your hosted account and associated profile, friendships, blocks, synced walks/routes/stats, sent/received server invites and device tokens. For an Apple account, the deletion flow also requests revocation of FogTrail's Apple authorization. It does not recall files or copies another person has already saved.

Clear local data deletes the device's stored walks, tracks, steps, fog, places, memories and walker profile and resets local examples. It does not delete a hosted account or its records. Deleting the app likewise removes its local container; it does not request account deletion. Exported files and copies on other devices remain wherever you stored or sent them.

Active server records remain until you delete them through the relevant controls or delete the account. Infrastructure backups and operational records may follow the provider's retention process; we do not offer account recovery after deletion. Subscription cancellation is separate and managed by Apple.

FogTrail Pro and Apple

Subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles payment and renewal. FogTrail checks the device's subscription entitlement; it does not operate a server-side purchase profile or receive your payment details. Manage subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use.

Young walkers

FogTrail is for ages 13 and over. Anyone under 19 needs a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Parents can request removal at the contact below. Share only with known, trusted people; use a Privacy Zone around home and school, and do not put private information in names or hints. Read Walking safely together.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your walker name, manage friendships and blocks, disable sync and notifications, export local data and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, deletion or a privacy question about data we hold, contact us. Identity verification may be needed to protect your account. This policy applies wherever FogTrail is offered.
