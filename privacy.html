Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

The short version. FogTrail has no account, no sign-in and no server. It contains no networking code at all — no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs. Your steps, your walks, your location while walking and the fog you have lifted stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We never receive any of it.

What FogTrail stores on your device

  • Walks — the date, duration, distance, elevation gain, which place you were walking, and how much fog that walk lifted.
  • Revealed map cells — which parts of a place you have cleared, so a cleared place stays cleared.
  • Daily step totals and your daily step goal.
  • App settings — such as whether you have completed onboarding and which step sources you have enabled.
  • The demo circle — Mara, Jun, Tess and Nico are seeded example walkers created by the app on first launch. They are not real users, their numbers are invented, and nothing about them is sent or received.

All of it lives in the app's container on your phone and is removed when you clear your data or delete the app.

Permissions FogTrail asks for

Location — While Using the App

FogTrail records your path only while a walk session is running. It is used to lift fog as you move, to keep you inside a place's area, and to work out the distance and elevation of your walk. Your path is saved to the walk on your device and is not transmitted.

Because a walk you have started should not stop because you put the phone in your pocket, recording continues while the screen is off for the length of that walk. iOS shows its blue location indicator the whole time. The moment you end the walk, background recording is switched off again. FogTrail never requests "Always" access — it only ever has When In Use — so outside a running walk it has no location access to use.

Apple Health — step count, read only

With your permission FogTrail reads your step count from Apple Health so your daily totals and step goal are accurate, including steps written to Health by an Apple Watch or another app. FogTrail never writes to Health. Health data is read on your device, displayed to you, and never leaves the phone.

In line with Apple's requirements, we confirm that health data obtained through HealthKit is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for data mining or any purpose other than showing you your own activity in the app.

Motion & Fitness

If Health access is unavailable or off, FogTrail falls back to the iPhone's pedometer for a daily step total. Same rule: on device, shown to you, not transmitted.

What we collect

Nothing. Because the app has no network code, no personal information is transmitted to us or to anyone else. We hold no account records, no profiles, no logs and no backups of your data. We cannot see your walks, your location or your health data, and we could not produce them if asked.

No tracking, no advertising

FogTrail does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to ask about.

Deleting your data

Open Me → Settings & privacy → Clear local data. This deletes every walk in your history, the revealed cells those walks earned, and your saved daily steps; the example places and demo circle are then planted again and your profile returns to its seeded starting numbers. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can revoke Location, Health or Motion access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

FogTrail is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Your rights

Privacy laws including Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's PIPA give you rights of access, correction and deletion over personal information an organisation holds about you. We hold none: your information never reaches us, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. The data is in your hands on your device, and the deletion control above is immediate. If you have a question about this, email us.

Where FogTrail is available

FogTrail is offered in selected territories outside the European Union and the European Economic Area. Wherever it is available, the answer above is the same one: the app has no network code, so nothing you do in it is transmitted to us or to anyone else, and no local privacy law can require us to disclose, correct or delete information we never receive.

Changes

If FogTrail ever gains a feature that sends data anywhere — an account, a real social circle, cloud backup — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you before any data leaves your phone.
