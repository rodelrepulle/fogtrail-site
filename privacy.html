Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The short version. Your steps, your walks, your location while walking, your health data and the fog you have lifted stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone. None of it is ever uploaded. FogTrail contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs.

If you sign in with Apple, FogTrail creates an account for you on our server. That account holds exactly two things: the name you chose to share and your avatar colour. It holds no walking data of any kind. Signing in is optional, the app works fully without it, and you can delete the account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

You may also subscribe to FogTrail Pro. That is handled by Apple on its own terms; we never see a payment detail.

What FogTrail stores on your device

  • Walks — the date, duration, distance, elevation gain, the path you walked, which place you were walking, and how much fog that walk lifted.
  • Revealed map cells — which parts of a place you have cleared, so a cleared place stays cleared.
  • Daily step totals and your daily step goal.
  • Your walker name and avatar colour — whatever you type, or the first name Apple gives us if you sign in with Apple. A copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline; if you are signed in, these two values are also stored in your account, and they are the only things that are.
  • Places you keep, and photos you attach to them — if you turn a finished walk into a place of your own, the photo you choose is copied into the app's storage as a small image.
  • Places sent to you — a .fogtrail file a friend hands you through Messages, AirDrop, Mail or Files becomes a fogged card in the app. It travels between two phones directly; it never passes through our server.
  • App settings — such as whether you have completed onboarding and which step sources you have enabled.
  • The demo circle — Mara, Jun, Tess and Nico are seeded example walkers created by the app on first launch. They are not real users, their numbers are invented, and nothing about them is sent or received.

All of it lives in the app's container on your phone and is removed when you clear your data or delete the app.

Signing in with Apple

The first screen offers Continue with Apple, and you can skip it — "Continue without an account" is always there, and nothing in the app is locked behind signing in.

If you do use it, Apple shows you its own sheet and asks whether to share your name. FogTrail asks Apple for your name and nothing else — in particular we never request your email address, so no address, real or private-relay, is given to us. The name is written to your walker card on this phone and is used for one thing: labelling you on the board and on any place you choose to send.

Signing in also creates your FogTrail account, which stores that name and your avatar colour so the card follows you. Nothing else is stored with it — no walks, no routes, no steps, no health data, no places. You can delete the account at any time with Me → Settings & privacy → Delete account.

Your relationship with Apple during that sheet is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can review or stop app access to your Apple Account at any time in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. Clearing your data in FogTrail (below) removes the name from this phone; deleting your account (also below) removes it from our server and asks Apple to revoke access as well.

FogTrail Pro — subscriptions

FogTrail Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the entire transaction. FogTrail never sees or stores your payment details, your Apple Account, a receipt, a purchase history or any identifier from the purchase. The app asks Apple a single question — is there an active subscription on this device right now? — and shows or hides the Pro features on the answer. Nothing about your purchase is recorded by us or sent anywhere by us.

Your purchase, its renewal and any refund are between you and Apple, under Apple's Media Services terms. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See the Terms of Use for the plan lengths and renewal terms.

Permissions FogTrail asks for

Location — While Using the App

FogTrail records your path only while a walk session is running. It is used to lift fog as you move, to keep you inside a place's area, and to work out the distance and elevation of your walk. Your path is saved to the walk on your device and is not transmitted.

Because a walk you have started should not stop because you put the phone in your pocket, recording continues while the screen is off for the length of that walk. iOS shows its blue location indicator the whole time. The moment you end the walk, background recording is switched off again. FogTrail never requests "Always" access — it only ever has When In Use — so outside a running walk it has no location access to use.

Apple Health — step count, read only

With your permission FogTrail reads your step count from Apple Health so your daily totals and step goal are accurate, including steps written to Health by an Apple Watch or another app. FogTrail never writes to Health. Health data is read on your device, displayed to you, and never leaves the phone.

In line with Apple's requirements, we confirm that health data obtained through HealthKit is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for data mining or any purpose other than showing you your own activity in the app.

Motion & Fitness

If Health access is unavailable or off, FogTrail falls back to the iPhone's pedometer for a daily step total. Same rule: on device, shown to you, not transmitted.

What we collect

If you do not sign in: nothing. There is no account, and nothing leaves your phone.

If you sign in with Apple, we store one row on our server describing you as a walker:

  • Your name — the name you chose to share on Apple's sheet, which you can edit or blank at any time.
  • Your avatar colour — a single number.
  • An account identifier — a random ID linking that row to your Sign in with Apple identity, so the app knows the account is yours.

That is the complete list. We do not receive or store your walks, your routes, your location, your steps, your health data, the fog you have lifted, the places you make, or the photos you attach to them. None of it is sent to us, and we could not produce any of it if asked.

We never ask Apple for your email address. FogTrail requests only your name from Sign in with Apple, so no address — real or private-relay — is given to us. If you use the app's own Continue with email option instead, the address is used on your device to suggest a name and is not saved.

Subscribing is a transaction between you and Apple. We are not given an advertising identifier, a device identifier, a receipt or a payment detail.

Your account row is stored on infrastructure operated for us by Supabase in Singapore. It is protected by row-level security, so the only account that can read or change your row is yours.

No tracking, no advertising

FogTrail does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because there is nothing to ask about.

Taking your data with you

Open Me → Settings & privacy → Export my walks. FogTrail writes your finished walks and saved daily step totals to a JSON file on the device and hands it to the iOS share sheet, so you decide where it goes — Files, a message, or nowhere at all. The file is created locally and is not uploaded by the app. A place you have not cleared appears in the file by its hint rather than its name, exactly as it does in the app.

Deleting your account

If you signed in, open Me → Settings & privacy → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account row — your name, your avatar colour and the identifier linking them to you — and asks Apple to revoke FogTrail's access to your Apple Account. It cannot be undone, and we keep no backup from which to restore it.

Deleting your data on the device

Open Me → Settings & privacy → Clear local data. This deletes every walk in your history, the path each one recorded, the revealed cells those walks earned, your saved daily steps, the places you made or were sent, the photos you attached to them, and your walker name — including the name Apple gave us if you signed in with Apple. The example places and demo circle are then planted again and your profile returns to its seeded starting numbers.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can revoke Location, Health or Motion access at any time in the iOS Settings app, and you can stop FogTrail's access to your Apple Account in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Clearing your data does not cancel a subscription, because we do not hold your subscription — Apple does. Cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Children

FogTrail is not directed at children and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. The only personal information an account holds is a name you choose, which need not be your real one; if you believe a child has created an account, delete it in the app or contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Privacy laws including Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's PIPA give you rights of access, correction and deletion over personal information an organisation holds about you. If you have not signed in we hold nothing about you at all. If you have, we hold only the name, avatar colour and account identifier described above — you can read them on your walker card, correct the name in the app, and delete all of it immediately with Delete account. Your walks, location and health data never reach us, so there is nothing there for us to disclose, correct or delete. If you have a question about this, email us.

Where FogTrail is available

FogTrail is offered in selected territories outside the European Union and the European Economic Area. Wherever it is available, the answer above is the same one: your walking is never transmitted to us, and the only information we hold is the account row described above — which you can read, correct and delete yourself from inside the app. If you sign in, that row is stored in Singapore.

Changes

This policy was rewritten on 6 September 2026, when FogTrail gained hosted accounts: before that version there was no server and no account at all. If FogTrail gains a further feature that sends data anywhere — a real social circle, cloud backup of your walks — this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you before any walking data leaves your phone.
