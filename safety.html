Walking safely

Last updated: September 2, 2026

FogTrail is not a navigation or emergency tool

Do not rely on FogTrail to find a route, to find your way back, or to get help. It does not do turn-by-turn navigation, it does not work without a screen you are looking at, and it cannot contact anyone for you. Carry a real map, a charged phone, and the means to call for help.

The places are real. The detail is illustrative.

FogTrail ships with a small set of example places around Vancouver's North Shore and East Vancouver so the fog mechanic works on a real walk. The coordinates are real, but the drawn line is not a surveyed trail. It is a shape generated so the fog has something to lift. The difficulty, surface, amenity and note fields are example values written for the app, not a report from anyone who walked there. The photographs are illustrative artwork, not pictures of those locations.

Treat all of it as decoration on top of real ground. Get your actual trail information from the park authority that manages the land.

Look up

FogTrail lifts fog while a walk is running and the app is on screen. That is a real tension: the app asks for your attention exactly when the ground deserves it. Put the phone away on rough, steep or exposed sections. A trail you did not clear today will still be there tomorrow.

Steep ground on the North Shore

Several of the example places sit on or near terrain that generates real search-and-rescue calls every year. Rain, snow, mud, root and rock, and early winter darkness change these walks completely. Before you go:

  • Check the park's own trail report and closures.
  • Check the forecast and the sunset time, then plan to be out well before it.
  • Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • Carry water, a layer, a light, and food.
  • Turn back rather than push on. This is a game about fog, not about summiting.

Health and step data

Step counts, distance and elevation shown in FogTrail are estimates from your phone's sensors or from Apple Health. They are not medical measurements and are not a fitness or health assessment. If you have a condition that walking could affect, talk to a clinician rather than to a leaderboard.

In an emergency

Call your local emergency number. In British Columbia that is 911, and search and rescue is dispatched through it. Do not attempt to use FogTrail to report or locate an incident.
