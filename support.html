Support

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Email support@cairnstudio.si with your app version, what happened and the screen involved. Avoid sending an exact route, private address, health export or device token unless needed and requested.

Walking and location

Start a walk to record and lift fog. It continues with the screen locked and stops when you end it. FogTrail never requests Always location. Outside a walk, Current Location takes one temporary fix for distances or the Privacy Zone picker. The four seeded Lower Mainland places have illustrative routes and artwork. Walks elsewhere still uncover your own map; a kept personal place can be sent. Read Walking safely.

Steps and hikes

Allow Apple Health step-count access, or Motion & Fitness for the phone's pedometer. Health denied or empty falls back to Motion. Distance, elevation, splits and step totals are sensor estimates. A hike lens is activity detail, not navigation.

Real friends and example walkers

Sign in to use real Circle friends. Share a single-use friend link, then accept a request before exchanging places. There is no public directory, feed or chat. Mara, Jun, Tess and Nico are optional example walkers with invented totals; they are separate from real accepted friends. You can remove or block a real friend in Circle.

Sync choices and weekly boards

Walking statistics sync is off until you enable it. Route sync is a separate choice requiring statistics sync. Accepted friends see weekly totals, not your private routes or per-walk history. Routes are filtered around walk starts/ends and your Privacy Zone before upload. Turning walking sync off removes active synced walks/routes/stats; turning route sync off removes its route points. Restore merges the filtered copy; it cannot recover withheld areas or make a restored route into a new sendable place.

Sending and receiving places

Circle sends to an accepted friend and stores the package on our server. The inbox works without notifications; refresh or reopen Circle when online. This version sends the place's name, hint and filtered route without photos or personal notes. Files sent through Messages, AirDrop or Mail remain a separate option and can include attachments.

You can decline a received place and delete a sent place from the server. Removing or blocking a friend ends server access, but cannot erase a copy already saved or exported. Report unwanted or unsafe content by emailing support, and block the sender.

Notifications

Enable place notifications explicitly in Circle's sync settings. Permission denial does not prevent inbox delivery. Alerts contain generic text and open Circle. If enabled alerts do not arrive, check iOS Settings → Notifications → FogTrail and your connection. Push delivery is best effort and may be unavailable while server setup is incomplete. Turning off the in-app option removes this device's server token. A failed network removal is retried, including after session expiry; refresh when online.

Signing in

Apple sign-in requests your name only. Google supplies your name and email address. Continue with email merely suggests a local name; it creates no server account. You can continue without an account for local walking and discovery. See the Privacy Policy for the optional account and upload details.

Export, backup and deletion

Me → Settings & privacy offers walk export and a manual FogTrail backup. A backup is an exact private restore file and contains protected areas; FogTrail does not encrypt it. Keep it private. This differs from filtered optional cloud restore.

Clear local data removes this device's content; deleting the app does the same. Neither deletes the hosted account. Delete account removes the account and associated active server records. Copies saved by other people or exported to other services remain.

FogTrail Pro

FogTrail Pro is optional. Current plans, prices and renewal terms appear in the app before purchase and in the Terms of Use. Apple handles payment.

Cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is in the subscription screen and Me settings. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Clearing data or deleting an account does not cancel a subscription.

Watch, Garmin and Fitbit

There is no FogTrail Watch app or Garmin/Fitbit login. Their activity counts only when their apps write steps to Apple Health and you permit FogTrail to read them.
